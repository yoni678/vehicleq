# VehicleQ Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: October 5, 2026**

VehicleQ is designed to help users track vehicle expenses, mileage, vehicle value, and estimated ownership costs. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the VehicleQ mobile application.

## Information You Enter

VehicleQ allows you to enter information about your vehicles and vehicle-related activity. This may include vehicle year, make and model, mileage and odometer readings, purchase information, estimated vehicle value, expenses, expense notes, and mileage purpose.

## How Your Information Is Stored

In the current version of VehicleQ, information entered into the app is stored locally on your device. VehicleQ does not require a user account, and the current version does not transmit your vehicle records to VehicleQ-operated servers.

## Information Collection and Tracking

The current version of VehicleQ does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. VehicleQ does not currently request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photo library, or Bluetooth.

## How Information Is Used

Information entered into VehicleQ is used by the app to provide its features, including expense tracking, mileage tracking, vehicle valuation information entered by the user, and estimated vehicle ownership-cost calculations.

## Sharing of Information

The current version of VehicleQ does not sell or share the vehicle information you enter with advertisers or other third parties.

## Data Retention and Deletion

VehicleQ stores entered information locally on your device. You can delete individual expense and mileage records within the app, and you can delete a vehicle and its associated VehicleQ records using the app's vehicle deletion feature.

Removing the VehicleQ application from your device may also remove locally stored application data, subject to the behavior of your device, operating system, backups, and other services controlled by you or the platform provider.

## Data Security

VehicleQ is designed to minimize collection and transmission of personal information. However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Children's Privacy

VehicleQ is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through accounts or VehicleQ-operated servers. If future versions introduce features that change how information is collected or processed, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

VehicleQ may change as new features are introduced. This Privacy Policy may therefore be updated to reflect changes in the application's features, data practices, or legal requirements. The effective date at the top of this policy will indicate when it was last updated.
